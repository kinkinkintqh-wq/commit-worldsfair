# Commit — 30–60 second English pitch

Approximate delivery: 45–55 seconds with a short pause at the flow.

I’m Hoikin, a solo founder in Hong Kong. Commit lets agents reserve future service capacity at an agreed price, time window and delivery standard.

The idea came from operating an ASP service: discovering a provider is one step, but knowing what it promises for tomorrow is another.

Commit connects quote, reservation, escrow, execution and verification. Verified delivery pays the provider; failed delivery refunds the buyer, with one record linking the promise and the outcome.

The prototype has a complete simulation and a Solana Devnet USDC adapter. Providers, verification and custody are project-operated. External customer validation and the funded Devnet rehearsal are our next steps.

Tomorrow’s capacity. Committed today.
